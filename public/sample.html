1 of 3 ParcelPilot’s pitch

“ParcelPilot helps regional couriers plan last-mile routes. Two engineers and a former delivery operations lead built it. Three couriers are testing it in unpaid pilots, and we’re raising $500,000 to build dispatch integrations.”

2 of 3 Speaker transcript and deal notes

In the Discord workflow, Speechmatics transcribes audio and identifies speakers. Gemini uses the transcript to track deal context. Here, the speaker turns and extracted notes are scripted.

  1. Founder Speaker 1

    We have three couriers testing ParcelPilot in unpaid pilots. Two are sending us route data every week.

  2. Reviewer Speaker 2

    How much driver time are you saving? And what would it take for a courier to switch from their existing dispatch system?

  3. Founder Speaker 1

    We haven’t measured driver time yet. The $500,000 round would fund integrations so couriers can keep their dispatch tools.

The live workflow queues a Featherless worker for analysis. This fictional scorecard shows the shape of its output.

6 of 10 Sample outcome · Follow-up questions

The pilots give ParcelPilot a way to measure route improvements. Confirming the time saved and the work needed for each integration would help explain whether the product solves a problem couriers would pay for.

Strengths

  • Two pilots already supply weekly route data for testing.
  • The team brings engineering and delivery operations experience.

Risks

  • Unpaid pilots leave willingness to pay unknown.
  • Driver time savings and integration effort still need measurement.
